# Refund policy (synthetic eval document)

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, an annual plan is refunded
pro rata only if the service was unavailable for more than 24 hours in a calendar month.

Monthly plans are not refunded, but can be cancelled at any time and stay active until the end of the billing period.

Refund requests go through the billing page; the finance team answers within three business days.
