# Travel expense policy (synthetic eval document)

Daily meal limit: 55 euros per person per day while travelling. Receipts are required for any single meal above
15 euros.

Hotels: book through the approved portal; the nightly cap is 140 euros outside capital cities and 190 euros inside them.

Flights: economy class for trips under six hours. Trips of six hours or more may book premium economy with
manager approval.

Claims must be filed within 30 days of the trip's last day.
